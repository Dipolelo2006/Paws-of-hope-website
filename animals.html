Animals Looking for Forever Homes

These beautiful animals are currently in our care and looking for their forever homes. Each profile includes a short description, age, temperament and special notes. When you find an animal that touches your heart, please complete the adoption application form on our Get Involved page.

How Adoption Works

  1. Browse the available animals below
  2. Submit an adoption application
  3. We review your application and contact you
  4. Meet-and-greet at the shelter
  5. Home check (if required)
  6. Finalise adoption and welcome your new family member home
