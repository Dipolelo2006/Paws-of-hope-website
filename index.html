Welcome to Paws of Hope

Paws of Hope Animal Rescue is a registered non-profit organisation dedicated to giving abandoned and neglected animals a second chance at life. From our shelter on the East Rand we rescue, rehabilitate and carefully rehome dogs and cats while educating communities about responsible pet ownership.

Our Impact

  • 2 400+ animals rehomed since 2018
  • 1 800+ sterilisations performed in surrounding communities
  • 120+ animals currently in our care
  • 85 active volunteers and foster carers
